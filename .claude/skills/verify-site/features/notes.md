# Notes curator

## Sub-features

- Week list with status (draft, acknowledged, sent), subscriber metrics.
- The week editor: body, preview, auto-publish checkbox, "I'm happy with this".

## How to get to it (user POV)

Sign in, press "Notes" in the private header.

## Driving it with the browser pane

1. Navigate to `/editorial?workspace=notes`. Expect the local weeks or an empty state.
2. Open one week; the editor and preview show. Leave without pressing happy or send.

## Gotchas

- "I'm happy with this" can send the letter when auto-publish is off. Never press it during a check.
- Metrics need Resend keys; locally they show an empty or unavailable state.
