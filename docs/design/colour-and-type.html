● Reading in dark mode

A softer dark theme. About 10:1 to 14:1, not 16:1.

Near-white text on near-black glows at the edges of each letter, and the eye tires. Every dark page now sits on slightly lifted paper with dimmer ink. Long text (essay and Notes paragraphs) drops one more step to reading ink. Headings and bold text keep the ink.

Rules

  • An <em> in an h1 or h2 is the second tone: upright, in --ink-2, never italic.
  • Papaya is a signal: dots, the last step, focus. Orange text uses --leaf-ink.
  • Separate with 1px hairlines and space, not boxes. One raised object per view.
  • Do not bring back Instrument Serif or Manrope.
